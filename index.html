Full interactive site

Passcode login, RSVP, and themed pages need the SvelteKit app. After cloning: 

pnpm install
cp .env.example .env
# set WEDDING_PASSCODE and SESSION_SIGNING_SECRET in .env
pnpm run dev:netlify

Then open http://localhost:8888 and enter the passcode from your .env.
